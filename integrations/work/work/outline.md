# Outline: "The Lighthouse Keeper's Ledger"

Genre: literary, quiet magical realism. ~1500-2000 words per chapter. Third person past, close on Mara.

## Characters
- Mara Quell (34): returns to remote island of Skerrow after her estranged father Tobias, the lighthouse keeper, dies. Practical, guarded, works as an insurance auditor on the mainland.
- Tobias Quell (dead): kept a ledger of every ship the light "saved" — but entries continue after dates he could not have seen them.
- Ewan Bright (60s): ferryman, Tobias's only friend. Dry humor.

## Chapter 1 — The Ledger
Mara arrives by Ewan's ferry in autumn. Lighthouse is automated now; her father stayed anyway. She finds the ledger: decades of ship names, dates, "kept safe." The last entry is dated tomorrow. Ends: she reads tomorrow's name — "The Linnet" — Ewan's boat.

## Chapter 2 — The Storm
Mara tries to dismiss it; asks Ewan, who laughs it off but admits Tobias sometimes phoned him not to sail. Storm rises. Automated light fails (power cut). Mara climbs the tower, finds the old oil lamp her father maintained "just in case." Struggles to light it; memories of childhood, him teaching her. Ewan is out on the water bringing a medical supply run. She lights it.

## Chapter 3 — Kept Safe
Dawn. The Linnet made it in. Ewan says he saw the old light, yellow not white. Mara opens the ledger: a new entry in her own handwriting? No — blank lines after. She writes the entry herself: "The Linnet — kept safe." She decides to stay through winter. Final image: next page, a new date appears faintly, waiting. Theme: inheritance, choosing to keep watch.
